    Open by design

    This is an open agentic website. Its agent network is public JSON (creator_agent_studio.network.json, listed in agent-manifest.json) and runs entirely in your browser. Other agentic websites can call it at this-site/creator_agent_studio to get a brief, an outline and a thread for their own visitors. It also calls a partner site, Hook Lab, where a writer agent and a critic agent rank hooks for your topic; you see a card and choose before anything is sent there.

    It drafts; it does not publish, upload, schedule, or make images, video or voice. Check any fact marked [check] before you post.
